About

I build software that's meant to still be running in three years.

My work sits at the intersection of backend architecture, third-party integrations, and the less glamorous disciplines that make systems trustworthy — security, auditability, and upgrade paths that don't break production.

I think of good engineering as a series of small, defensible decisions: how data is modeled, what gets tracked versus what gets computed, where a system should be flexible and where it shouldn't. I'd rather ship something narrow and correct than broad and fragile.

Most of my recent work has involved IOT platforms, GRC (governance, risk & compliance) tooling, and integration-heavy systems — connecting third-party services cleanly, planning application upgrades without downtime, and making architectural calls that hold up when the stakes are compliance, not just uptime.
